Summary

  • No account. No analytics. No advertising. No crash-reporting services.
  • Everything you create in the app — places, photos, videos, notes and reports — is stored on your device in the app's private storage.
  • We (the publisher) do not receive your data. The app has no servers.
  • Data leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it (for example, emailing a report to your landlord).

    • What the app stores on your device

  • Details you enter: place name, address, landlord or manager name, notes, room names, issue descriptions, markers, comparison notes, and an optional name to print on reports.
  • Photos and videos you take in the app or choose from your library, stored as unchanged copies, plus smaller preview copies without camera metadata.
  • Information the app records automatically: the date and time (from your device clock) when each file was saved, file sizes, SHA-256 fingerprints, and, if present in a photo, the camera date, camera make/model and whether the photo contains location data. The app does not read or store location coordinates, but your original photos may contain location data written by your camera, and exported originals keep it.
  • Generated PDF reports and the date you mark a report as sent.

    • Permissions

  • Camera (iOS): to take photos/videos when you tap a capture button. On Android the app uses your phone's camera app and does not request the camera permission.
  • Photos (iOS): to add photos/videos you pick. On Android the system photo picker gives the app only the items you select.
  • Microphone (iOS): to record sound in videos you choose to record.

    • Sharing and exports

    When you use Share, Send to landlord or Export evidence package, the app hands the file to the app you choose (email, messaging, cloud storage). What happens next is governed by that app's and recipient's policies.

    Backups

  • iPhone: your device backups (iCloud or computer) may include the app's data, according to your settings.
  • Android: the app's data is excluded from Google cloud backup. Direct phone-to-phone transfer may copy it. Use "Export evidence package" to keep your own copy.

    • App stores

    Apple and Google may collect information about app downloads, updates and crashes under their own privacy policies. The app does not add any additional tracking.

    Retention and deletion

    Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual photos, issues, rooms, inspections, places and reports, or use Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the device (except copies you exported or shared, and device backups).
