# SOURCES

All sources accessed 2026-09-25.

**Method note (read first):** In this research session, the network egress proxy blocked WebFetch for every domain tried (census.gov, gov.uk, leginfo.legislature.ca.gov, app.leg.wa.gov, depositprotection.com, apps.apple.com, news.ycombinator.com, generationrent.org). Reddit was blocked for search too. **Every source below was therefore seen only as a search-engine result: a title, a URL, and a machine-generated summary or snippet.** No page was opened in full. Numbers attributed to a source are the numbers the search summary gave for that source. Anything that sets a threshold, like a price, rating, or statutory deadline, should be checked on the primary page before it goes into product copy or legal claims.

Source types: GOV-STAT (official statistics), STATUTE (statute or legislature text, or a mirror of it), GOV-GUIDE (government or court guidance), SCHEME (official deposit scheme or tribunal), NGO (advocacy or charity), COMMERCIAL-SURVEY (survey sponsored by a company), TRADE (trade association or law-firm blog), VENDOR (a competitor's own site or listing), AGGREGATOR (third-party stats or review site), FORUM (user-generated), NEWS.
