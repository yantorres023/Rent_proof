# JTBD — Jobs To Be Done (derived from desk evidence)

Date: 2026-09-25. **Every job below is UNVALIDATED_WITH_REAL_USERS.** I derived them from statutes, deposit-scheme guidance, surveys and forum titles; no interviews were done. Source numbers [n] refer to SOURCES.md.

## Core job statement

> When I get the keys to a new rental, I want to quickly capture the condition of every room in a way I can find and trust later, so that I don't get charged for damage or dirt that was already there when I move out.

Evidence basis:
- Forum titles about pre-existing damage and missing checklists [96–102].
- University legal offices telling renters to photograph at move-in [112–115].
- Scheme guidance that date-stamped photos are evidence [47].
- Zillow's correlational documentation-vs-refund figure [42].

## Job map by moment

1. **Pre-move-in** (lease signed): know what to capture, and know any state or UK deadline. Jobs F3, E4.
2. **Move-in** (first 0–72 h): guided capture, defect notes, export the objection list to the landlord. Jobs F1, F2, F3, E2, S1.
3. **During tenancy:** log maintenance issues. Job F5.
4. **Move-out:** repeat the shots and compare. Job F6. California renters can request a pre-move-out inspection [13][16].
5. **Post-move-out** (deduction received): rebut charges and assemble the evidence pack. Jobs F7, F8, E1, S3.

## Riskiest assumptions (to test first)

1. Renters will do a guided 20–40-minute walkthrough on move-in day, which is a busy and stressful moment (F1). This is the main behavioural risk.
2. A structured report beats the camera roll enough to matter in outcomes (F7). No causal data exists: Zillow's figure is correlational [42].
3. Renters will pay at the moment of need (F8), rather than at install.
