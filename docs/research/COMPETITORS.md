## 2. Per-competitor notes (selected)

- **Rentproof (getrentproof.app)** [86]. FACT: "one-time $4.99", "photos and data never leave your device", "no accounts, cloud storage, or tracking", "guided wizard", "compare move-in vs move-out", "legal-ready PDF reports". INFERENCE: this is essentially our current concept, already shipped on iOS. Risks: store search confusion, possible trademark dispute, and no differentiation. ACTION for the product team: run a name clearance (USPTO/UKIPO and store search) before any public launch.
- **MoveSnap** [87]. FACT: a same-angle comparison slider, a mid-tenancy event log, a "Dispute Evidence Bundle" pairing move-in and move-out photos with dates, GPS and signatures, and a QR code that verifies the report's generation time. INFERENCE: this sets the feature bar for tenant apps. Its QR verification implies a server component, which is a trade-off against our local-first approach.
- **DepositGenie** [89]. FACT: AI diffing, AI rebuttal letters, deadline tracking, "tamper-proof timestamps and hashes", and a free tier. INFERENCE: it competes on the dispute phase (letters, deadlines), which we do not cover yet.
- **Tenant Inspect** [88]. FACT: $19.99, with a third-party estimate of 3.9★ and about 300 downloads. INFERENCE: a paid-upfront tenant app got little traction. This is weak evidence that upfront payment limits adoption.
- **RentCheck** [63–67]. FACT: B2B paid per unit, Android 4.52★ from 3.5k ratings and ~89k downloads. Reviews and the vendor's help centre mention lost photos and upload problems. INFERENCE: many renters already meet "guided inspection" through their property manager. For them, the gap is **their own copy** of evidence that the landlord does not control, and reliability (offline, no lost photos).
- **Timestamp camera apps** [83]. FACT: ~90M downloads for one app. INFERENCE: this is the real incumbent behaviour. Renters want proof of time and do not look for an inspection app. Our pitch must beat "just use the camera" on organization and on producing a report.
- **Rhino / Obligo** [84][85]. INFERENCE: deposit alternatives do not remove damage liability; the renter is still billed. The need for documentation remains, and billing without a held deposit may even be harsher. This is a HYPOTHESIS; I did not verify it with claim data.
- **ProofMode / Truepic** [81][82]. FACT: C2PA-conformant signed capture exists, both open source and enterprise. INFERENCE: a future RentProof could embed C2PA-style signing. SHA-256 alone proves that a file has not changed since it was hashed. It does not prove when the photo was captured, unless the hash is anchored by a third party (for example, the eIDAS timestamps ProofSnap uses [93], or a notary).

## 3. Gap analysis

| Gap | Evidence | Who partly fills it | Opportunity for RentProof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Renter-owned, landlord-independent record | Landlord tools keep data in the landlord's account [63][79][80] | Tenant indie apps [86–89] | Table stakes; already covered by competitors |
| Cross-platform (Android) tenant app with guided flow + comparison + PDF | Rentproof and MoveSnap are iOS/iPhone-only as seen [86][87] | Tenant Inspect, DepositGenie, MoveProof (Android) [88][89][91] | Flutter Android + iOS parity is a modest differentiator |
| Credible provenance, beyond watermarks and EXIF | EXIF is weak if contested (vendor claim [60]); ProofMode/Truepic use C2PA [81][82]; ProofSnap uses eIDAS timestamps [93] | ProofSnap, DepositGenie (claims hashes) | Local SHA-256 manifest + optional third-party timestamp anchoring (e.g. RFC 3161 / OpenTimestamps), a HYPOTHESIS to evaluate. Do not over-claim "court-proof" |
| Statutory-deadline and objection-window help (VA 5 days, MA 15 days, GA dissent, BC 7 days) | [21][22][24][30] | DepositGenie (deadline reminders) [89] | "Send your objection list before day X" export: high value if state-accurate, but a legal-accuracy burden |
| Annotating a landlord's inventory or checklist (UK inventory, TurboTenant or Avail checklist) | UK inventory culture [47][70–72]; tenant fills in the landlord's checklist [79][80] | None seen explicitly | "Inventory companion" mode: import or photo the landlord's report and attach rebuttal photos. Runner-up market fit |
| Dispute-phase packaging (itemized charge rebuttal) | Generation Rent: 46% didn't know they could challenge [49] | DepositGenie (AI letter) | Structured charge-by-charge evidence pack; later phase |
| Traction / distribution | No tenant-first app showed large ratings volume in what I saw | None | The real gap is distribution, not features |

**Bottom line (INFERENCE, MEDIUM confidence).** The tenant-first category is feature-saturated and traction-poor. At least 8 tenant-side apps exist, with one-time pricing of $4.99–$19.99 where visible. None shows scale in the data I could access. RentProof's defensible angles are narrow:
- Android + iOS parity.
- Local-first privacy combined with verifiable provenance (hash manifest + optional timestamp anchoring).
- Deadline and objection-window workflows.
- Distribution partnerships (universities, tenant unions).

The name "RentProof" conflicts with an existing app [86].
