# NAME CHECK — "RentProof" and alternatives

_2026-09-25. Web-search conflict screening only. **This is NOT trademark clearance.** A USPTO search (https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search [96]) was not performed: the tool is not reachable or queryable from this session. Before any public use, have a trademark attorney run a USPTO knockout search and a full search (including common-law and app-store uses) for the chosen name. Sources → `SOURCES_REDTEAM.md`._

## 1. "RentProof" / "Rent Proof": conflicts found

| Use | Same field? | Evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Rentproof" iOS app** (getrentproof.app): security-deposit protection, room-by-room timestamped photos, move-in vs move-out side-by-side, PDF, local-only, $4.99 | **Identical product, identical market (US renters)** | [59] | **Blocking** |
| **RentProof Inc.** (Toronto, rentproof.co): rent reporting for credit building, aimed at renters | Same audience (renters), adjacent service | [60] | High |
| **RentProof AI**: App Store listing (India storefront) | Rental property inspection/condition | [62][63] | High |
| **RentersProof** (rentersproofapp.com): renter documentation plus rights Q&A | Near-identical name, same market | [64] | High |
| **RENT PROOF LTD**: UK Companies House registration | Unknown activity | [61] | Low–Medium |

**Conclusion (INFERENCE).** At least one live app has used "Rentproof" for the *same* goods and services, which gives it common-law priority arguments in the US regardless of registration. There is also an App Store naming collision and a same-audience Canadian company. **Keeping "RentProof" risks store rejection, name confusion, a takedown or dispute, and weak SEO (we would be ranking against the incumbent's brand).** The name also invites a "proof" claim we must not make (LEGAL_CONTEXT §5).

## 2. Alternatives screened (quick web conflict check, one search each)

| Candidate | Conflicts found | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| **MoveProof** | Multiple App Store apps and a Google Play app named MoveProof doing renter move-in/out reports, plus MOVEPROOF LLC (FL, 2026) [65][66] | **Reject.** Crowded, identical field |
| **Walkthru** / WalkThru | MyWalkThru (tenant walkthrough reports) [74], WalkThruInspections.com [101] | **Reject** |

| **Move Ledger** / **MoveLedger** | No exact match surfaced. Results were generic rent-ledger apps | **Candidate.** Broader (fits move-in, move-out and maintenance timeline) |
| **Condition Ledger** | No exact match surfaced | Candidate, but clunky |
| **Unit Ledger** | No exact match. "Rent Ledger"/"Tenant Ledger" products exist [search], so it could be confused with accounting | Weak |

**Caveat.** "No conflict surfaced in one web search" is weak evidence. App Store and Play store searches, domain availability, social handles and USPTO were **not** checked.

## 3. Recommendation
**Switch names before any public artifact** (store listing, domain, social handle, landing page). Keep "RentProof" only as an internal codename.

The provisional front-runner is **"Move Ledger"**. It avoids implying legal proof, covers the pivot's scope (move-in → maintenance → move-out timeline), and no direct conflict surfaced. **"Deposit Ledger"** is the runner-up (clearer benefit, narrower scope). Next steps:
1. Check exact-match App Store and Play search results and domains (.app/.com).
2. Run a USPTO knockout in classes 9 (downloadable software) and 42 (SaaS), if applicable.
3. Get an attorney's opinion before spending on branding.
