# Brand

_2026-09-25. See research/NAME_CHECK.md and DECISIONS D-004._

## Name
- **Internal codename:** RentProof. Used in the repository, Dart package and docs. **Do not ship it**: an iOS app named "Rentproof" (getrentproof.app) sells nearly the same product, and a Toronto business called "RentProof" also exists.
- **Provisional public name:** **Move-In Record.**
  - Descriptive and low conflict risk.
  - Has search value for "move in" queries.
  - Weak as a trademark, because descriptive names are hard to protect.
- **Candidates checked and rejected:**
  - Move Ledger: "Ledger" is a prominent crypto-wallet brand in app stores.
  - RoomPrint: an existing web room planner.
  - RoomTrace: an existing iOS LiDAR app.
  - MoveProof, KeyDay, Walkthru: taken, per NAME_CHECK.md.
  - Deposit-something names: DepositGenie, DepositKit, Deposit Guard and Deposit Defender are all taken.
- **Recommendation:** have the owner pick a distinctive, coined name and run a USPTO search plus counsel review before any store listing or domain purchase (HUMAN_ACTION). Keep "Move-In Record" as the store subtitle or keyword phrase.
- **No formal trademark clearance was performed.**

## Positioning
For renters who want a fair start and a fair finish: a guided walkthrough that turns move-in day into a dated, organized condition report you send to your landlord, then compare at move-out. It is private, free, and honest about what it can and cannot show.

## Taglines (pick one; test later)
1. "Record it on day one."
2. "Your move-in, on the record."
3. "Walk through. Write it down. Send it."

## Voice
- Calm, plain, practical. Short sentences, like a friend who has moved many times.
- Honest about limits. Never promises money back or legal outcomes, never uses fear-mongering ("landlords will steal your deposit!").
- Say "record", "report", "fingerprint", "saved by the app". Avoid "proof", "court-ready", "tamper-proof", "guarantee".

## Visual direction
- Material 3. Primary blue #1F5AA6 (trust, paperwork). Success green #1E7A3C / #7ED69A (dark). Warning amber #9A5B00.
- Rounded outlined cards, generous spacing, large bottom buttons.
- Report PDF: three labeled styles (blue ORIGINAL MEDIA RECORD, amber USER NOTES, gray APP-GENERATED METADATA), Roboto, A4.

## App icon brief
- A simple house outline in white with a green check inside, on a full-bleed brand-blue square. Readable at 29 px.
- A placeholder that follows this brief is generated by `tool/generate_icons.py` (Android mipmaps, the iOS AppIcon set, and 1024/512 store icons in `release/assets/`).
- Final icon: commission a designer once the final name is chosen. Also provide an Android adaptive icon (foreground/background layers) and a monochrome themed icon.
- Avoid shields, gavels and locks, which imply legal protection or security guarantees.
