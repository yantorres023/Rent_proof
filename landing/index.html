Move-in day is when your record matters most

At move-out, the hard question is often: was it like that when you moved in? Photos scattered across a camera roll and chat threads are hard to find and hard to explain years later. Legal-aid and university housing guides commonly tell renters to document condition at move-in. Move-In Record makes that guided and organized.

How it works

  1. Add your place
    Name it; the address is optional.
  2. Walk each room
    Short prompts: walls, floor, windows, appliances.
  3. Note issues
    Describe damage and mark the spot on the photo.
  4. Get the report
    A PDF with every room, photo, note and date.
  5. Send & keep
    Share with your landlord and export a backup.

What you get

Guided, room by room

10 room types with simple photo prompts. Add your own rooms and prompts, or mark items "not in this unit".

Issues where they belong

Record the type and how noticeable each issue is, link it to a photo, and place a numbered marker.

A report you can send

A clean PDF you can email from your phone. The app notes the date you marked it as sent.

Originals kept unchanged

Each original file is stored as received, with a SHA-256 fingerprint you can check with standard tools.

A backup you control

Export an evidence package (.zip) with your originals, the report and a list of fingerprints.

Move-out, side by side

Repeat the same walkthrough when you leave and compare each room with your move-in photos.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Everything is stored in the app on your phone and goes nowhere unless you share or export it. Uninstalling the app deletes its data.

Honest about what it can and can't show

Dates come from your phone's clock at the moment the app saved each file. A fingerprint shows a stored file hasn't changed since. It doesn't prove when a photo was taken or who took it. Move-In Record isn't legal advice and can't guarantee any outcome. Rules and deadlines vary, so check your lease and local rules.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. The current version has no purchases and no ads.

Do I need my landlord's permission or participation?

No. You create your own record. Sending it to your landlord gives both of you the same information early.

Where are my photos stored?

In the app's private storage on your phone. Use "Export evidence package" to keep a copy somewhere safe, such as your email or cloud storage.

Will this get my deposit back?

No app can promise that. It helps you keep an organized, dated record and share it. How it's used is up to you, your landlord, and the rules where you live.

I already moved in. Is it too late?

You can still record the current condition, or import photos you took earlier (they're labeled as imported). You can also use it at move-out.

Android or iPhone?

Both.
