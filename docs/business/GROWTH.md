## 1. Why organic only
**INFERENCE from [79][80]:** at a ~$2.90 utilities CPI and ~2.2% freemium download-to-paid, cost per payer is about $130, against about $8.50 net revenue. Paid UA is off the table until measured otherwise.

## 2. Timing is the whole game
Renters care most at move-out/dispute, but evidence must be created at move-in (RED_TEAM 1.12). Channels must reach renters **between lease signing and the end of week 1**, and again **30–45 days before lease end**.

## 5. Landing-page copy (draft; no testimonials, no numbers, no guarantees)

> **Document your rental in one walkthrough. Send it the same day.**
>
> [AppName] guides you room by room through your new place, so you photograph every wall, floor, appliance and existing scratch *before* it can become your problem. Then it builds a clean PDF report you can email to your landlord right away.
>
> - **Guided, room by room.** A checklist that reminds you of closets, under sinks, inside the oven, windows and floors.
> - **Notes where they belong.** Mark issues on each photo while you're standing there.
> - **A report you can send today.** One tap emails the PDF to your landlord (and a copy to you), creating a dated record outside your phone.
> - **Your state's timeline.** See the move-in and move-out dates that apply where you live, with links to the official law, plus reminders.
> - **Move-out, side by side.** When you leave, walk the same rooms again and compare before and after.
> - **Private by design.** Your photos stay on your phone. No account needed.
>
> Free to document your move-in. Pay once per move for the move-out comparison pack. No subscription.
>
> _[AppName] is not a law firm and doesn't provide legal advice. It helps you organize your own records. Whether a landlord or court accepts them is up to them._

## 8. Referral hypothesis
- **HYPOTHESIS.** Roommates are a natural referral path: shared units need one inspection, but every roommate wants a copy. A "share inspection with roommate" export (PDF/archive) spreads the app by word of mouth without accounts or a server.
- **Second path.** A renter who wins or avoids a dispute tells friends. The only lever is a subtle "shared with [AppName]" footer on the PDF, where the landlord and roommates see it. Test whether the footer drives installs. Allow removing it in the paid pack.
- **Metric.** Installs from "PDF footer" or roommate share (store campaign links) as a share of total.

## 9. Creator partnership hypothesis
- **HYPOTHESIS.** Apartment-tour, "first apartment" and moving-day creators could show a guided move-in walkthrough as practical content. The free core makes this an honest recommendation (nothing to buy to follow along).
- **Test later** (requires outreach, not done): unpaid or affiliate-only partnerships with 3–5 micro-creators around the Aug–Sep lease season. Track via store campaign links.
- **Guardrails.** Creators may not claim guaranteed deposits or "court-proof" evidence. FTC disclosure is required for any compensation.
